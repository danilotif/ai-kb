# Harm evals & frontier-risk frameworks

How frontier labs measure and gate the dangerous capabilities of their models. Names vary, structure converges.

## The frameworks

*(Anthropic Responsible Scaling Policy (RSP); OpenAI Preparedness Framework; DeepMind Frontier Safety Framework; Meta Frontier AI Framework)*

## Risk categories

*(CBRN uplift; cyber-offense capability; autonomous replication / persuasive AI; model self-exfiltration in agentic settings)*

## Capability levels

*(tiered: ASL-1/2/3/4 (Anthropic); High/Critical (OpenAI); each level triggers additional safeguards)*

## Triggering mitigations

*(crossing a threshold → enhanced security, deployment restrictions, more red-teaming, potentially halt)*

## Standard evals

*(WMDP for hazardous knowledge; Cybench / SWE-bench-style for cyber; biosecurity sequence-design tests; agentic-replication evals like METR)*

## Pre-deployment testing with AISIs

*(US AISI, UK AISI; voluntary access agreements; results occasionally published)*

## Limits

*(evals are sampling adversarial capability, not certifying it absent; capability often crosses threshold without anyone noticing if not measured)*

## Open-weight tension

*(once weights are released, mitigations can be removed by fine-tuning; how much that matters is contested)*
