# Safety & alignment

Making models do what their developers and users want, refuse what they shouldn't, and behave predictably under adversarial input. Bigger than RLHF — but RLHF lives under `fine-tuning/`.

## What "alignment" covers

*(intent alignment: do what's asked; norm alignment: refuse harmful; capability alignment: don't be deceptive about what you're doing)*

## The current toolkit

*(RLHF/DPO post-training, constitutional AI, RLAIF, dual-use evals, classifier guards, red-teaming)*

## Failure modes

*(jailbreaks, prompt injection, sycophancy, hallucination as a safety issue, reward hacking, deceptive alignment)*

## Interpretability as a complement

*(probing, sparse autoencoders, circuits; not a deployment defense yet but the long-term hope)*

## Frontier-model risk frameworks

*(Anthropic RSP, OpenAI Preparedness, DeepMind Frontier Safety; CBRN, cyber, autonomy capabilities thresholds)*

## Red-teaming as practice

*(internal exercises, external bug bounties, evals; pointer to red-teaming.md)*

## Open questions
