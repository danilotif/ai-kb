# Open-weight frontier

The strongest open-weight models available right now. Pointer page; per-family deep dives live in `local-models/theory/open-weight-landscape.md`.

## DeepSeek line

*(DeepSeek V3.x base, DeepSeek-R1.x reasoning; strong on code, math; MIT-licensed weights; ~400 GB at int4 for full model)*

## Qwen line

*(Qwen 3 dense (0.6B → 72B), Qwen 3 MoE (30B-A3B, 235B-A22B); strong on code, multilingual; Apache 2.0 on most)*

## Llama line

*(Llama 4 MoE (Scout, Maverick, Behemoth); custom permissive license with size carve-outs)*

## Mistral

*(Small, Medium, Large recent; mix of Apache 2.0 and Mistral Research License; smaller dense models still competitive)*

## Gemma

*(Gemma 3, distilled from Gemini; 2B–27B; custom Gemma Terms; strong reasoning per parameter)*

## Specialty leaders

*(Qwen Coder for code; DeepSeek-Math; OLMo for fully-open research; Phi for tiny rigs)*

## How they compare to closed frontier

*(within 5pp on most benchmarks; gap remains on the hardest reasoning, agentic coding, frontier multimodal)*

## What's likely next

*(more MoE at the high end; more aggressive RLVR post-training; more native multimodal in open)*
