# EU AI Act

First major comprehensive AI law. Risk-based tiering, GPAI obligations, phased timeline.

## Structure

*(prohibited practices / high-risk systems / limited-risk / minimal-risk; plus a separate GPAI track for general-purpose models)*

## Prohibited practices

*(social scoring, manipulative techniques, real-time biometric ID with carve-outs)*

## High-risk systems

*(employment, education, critical infrastructure, law enforcement, migration; conformity assessments, registration, human oversight)*

## GPAI obligations

*(transparency: training-data summary, copyright policy, model card; evaluations and adversarial testing; cybersecurity)*

## Systemic-risk GPAI

*(>10^25 FLOPs threshold; additional obligations: risk assessments, serious-incident reporting, state-of-the-art evals)*

## Open-weight carve-out

*(GPAI obligations lighter for open-weight unless systemic-risk threshold crossed)*

## Timeline

*(Aug 2024 entry into force; Feb 2025 prohibitions live; Aug 2025 GPAI rules live; Aug 2026 most other obligations live; Aug 2027 high-risk in regulated products)*

## Penalties

*(up to €35M or 7% of global turnover for prohibited practices; tiered down for other violations)*

## What it means in practice

*(model cards become legally load-bearing; eval logs need to be documentation-grade; copyright opt-out signals have to be honored)*
