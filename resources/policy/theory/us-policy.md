# US AI policy

Patchwork — federal executive orders, agency guidance, state laws. Sectoral rather than horizontal.

## Federal executive orders

*(Biden-era EO 14110, subsequent rescissions and replacements; reporting thresholds for frontier training runs)*

## NIST AI Risk Management Framework

*(voluntary but the de facto US baseline; govern/map/measure/manage taxonomy)*

## US AI Safety Institute (USAISI)

*(pre-deployment evals with frontier labs; voluntary commitments; status under different administrations)*

## State laws

*(California SB 1047 saga; Colorado AI Act (2026); New York, Illinois, Texas with sector-specific rules)*

## Sectoral regulators

*(FTC on deceptive AI practices, FDA on AI medical devices, SEC on AI-washing disclosure, EEOC on hiring AI, CFPB on lending)*

## Litigation as policy

*(NYT v OpenAI, Authors Guild, Getty v Stability; courts setting fair-use boundaries)*

## What's stable vs unstable

*(antitrust scrutiny, sectoral rules, copyright lawsuits — stable; horizontal AI law and federal frontier-eval mandates — moving target)*
