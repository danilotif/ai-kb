# Export controls

US restrictions on AI compute and (some) model weights flowing out of the country. Reshape where models can be trained and served.

## What's controlled

*(advanced GPUs (H100, B200, B100, GB200), HBM, certain compiler/networking IP, model weights above compute thresholds)*

## Geography

*(China, Russia, Iran, North Korea fully restricted; Tier 2 countries with caps; Tier 1 (close allies) largely unrestricted)*

## Compute thresholds

*(reporting at 10^25 training FLOPs; tighter restrictions above 10^26; thresholds revised over time)*

## Model-weight controls

*(closed weights of frontier models above thresholds gated; open-weight position contested — cat-out-of-bag arguments vs national security arguments)*

## Diffusion (chip) rule

*(country-tier caps on H100-equivalents; affects cloud capacity in places like Singapore, UAE)*

## Practical effects

*(China-domiciled labs build on H800 / 910C / domestic; Western frontier training concentrated in US/UK/EU; cloud providers re-route GPU allocations)*

## Where it's likely to go

*(more granular tiers, more frequent revisions, push-back from allies on compute caps, potential expansion to model weights)*
