# Policy & regulation

The legal/regulatory backdrop AI products operate in. Mostly drafted faster than it can be implemented; expect churn.

## The major regimes

*(EU AI Act, US executive orders + state laws (CA SB 1047 era), UK pro-innovation, China generative AI rules)*

## What gets regulated

*(general-purpose model providers, high-risk deployers, transparency obligations, evals/red-team requirements)*

## Risk tiers

*(prohibited / high-risk / limited / minimal in EU; computational thresholds in US (10^25, 10^26 FLOPs))*

## Open-weight position

*(EU exempts open-weight unless GPAI-with-systemic-risk; US has favored disclosure but actively debated)*

## Copyright

*(training-data lawsuits, output-similarity claims, opt-out signals, fair-use posture varies by jurisdiction)*

## Export controls

*(US BIS rules on chip exports (H100, B200) and model weights above thresholds; effects on China, Singapore re-export)*

## Safety-related obligations

*(red-team disclosure, evals against bio/cyber/CBRN, reporting to AISI/AISIs, watermarking and provenance)*

## What this implies for builders

*(transparency artifacts: model cards, training-data summaries, eval logs; compliance becomes table stakes for shipping in EU)*
