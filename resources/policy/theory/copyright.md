# Copyright & training data

The largest unresolved legal question for the AI industry. What's been argued, what's been decided, and where it's headed.

## The core arguments

*(training is fair use / transformative — labs; training is reproduction at scale — rights holders; output similarity → infringement when occurring)*

## US litigation snapshot

*(NYT v OpenAI, Authors Guild v OpenAI, Getty v Stability, Concord Music v Anthropic, Andersen v Stability)*

## Key rulings so far

*(Bartz v Anthropic — fair-use friendly on training, separate question on legality of source data; Kadrey v Meta — early dismissals of some claims)*

## Output infringement

*(memorization-based claims; near-verbatim regeneration; image-style imitation; defenses — output filters, near-dup classifiers)*

## Opt-out mechanisms

*(robots.txt, ai.txt, X-Robots-Tag, individual rights-holder portals; legally non-binding but practically respected by major labs)*

## EU/UK position

*(Text and Data Mining exception with rights-reservation opt-out; cleaner statutory baseline than US fair-use bargaining)*

## Practical implications for products

*(provenance tracking, source attribution, takedown mechanisms, indemnification clauses in API ToS)*
